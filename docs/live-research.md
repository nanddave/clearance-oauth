# Live research runs

Clearance researches apps outside its reviewed library on request. These guides were produced on the live site; each is labelled agent-generated and still needs a developer to test a real connection.

| App | Time | Cost | Composio's app | Your own app |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Notion](https://clearance-oauth.vercel.app/g/notion) | 223 s | $0.46 | Can test now: Composio manages the OAuth app | Self-serve, same day: create a public connection in the Developer portal and copy the client ID, secret and authorization URL. |
| [Calendly](https://clearance-oauth.vercel.app/g/calendly) | 68 s | $0.47 | Can test now | Self-serve, same day: create a developer account and register a production OAuth app with a redirect URI and scopes. |
| [HubSpot](https://clearance-oauth.vercel.app/g/hubspot) | 118 s | $0.65 | Can test now | Self-serve, same day: create a developer account and the OAuth app, then install it in a HubSpot account as a Super Admin. |

Each claim shows its source and how it was checked. Of the 33 claims, 20 are backed by a word-for-word quote from the provider's own documentation (Notion 8 of 12, Calendly 2 of 9, HubSpot 10 of 12); the rest are marked reported or unverified on the page.

## Limits
- Composio coverage comes from Composio's toolkit catalog at research time; if the lookup fails, the guide says coverage is unknown.
- A review step needs an official, word-for-word source saying the provider reviews apps. Without one, the step is left out and the open question is shown instead.
- No run used a developer's connected account. Whether a given account can connect and run a tool is confirmed only by the local connection check.
